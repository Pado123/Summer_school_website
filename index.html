Fifteenth European Big Data Management & Analytics Summer School (eBISS 2027)

Introduction

We are pleased to announce the Fifteenth European Business Intelligence & Big Data Summer School. This summer school, presented by leading researchers in the field, represents an opportunity for postgraduate and Ph.D. students to equip themselves with the theoretical, practical, and collaboration skills necessary for developing challenging Data and Business Intelligence applications.

eBISS 2027 will take place from July 5 to July 9, 2027 in Padova, Italy, at the Department of Mathematics “Tullio Levi-Civita” of the University of Padova.

Approach

The summer school will provide a stimulating and enjoyable environment in which participants will benefit not only from the formal and practical sessions but also from informal and social interactions with established researchers and the other participants in the school. The electronic copy of all course lectures will be available to participants through the web site.

Participants

We welcome applicants from anywhere in the world. Normally, but not exclusively, applicants will be postgraduate and Ph.D. students in relevant disciplines with some knowledge of data management and analytics.

Accepted candidates are expected to participate fully in the activities of the school, from its start on July 5 until it ends on July 9. We will not consider applications from students who cannot commit themselves to full participation for the whole duration of the school.

Participants can also bring a poster (A1 paper format) describing their own research to the summer school, and thus take advantage of the audience for presenting their current research/tool in the area.

For any information concerning the organization of the summer school, please contact the local organizers.

Funding Organizations

The summer school is an event funded by the European Commission for the Erasmus Mundus Joint Master's Degree program “Big Data Management and Analytics” (BDMA).
