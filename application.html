Fifteenth European Big Data Management & Analytics Summer School (eBISS 2027)

Application to eBISS 2027

Applications are not open yet The application form and the application deadlines will be published on this page.

There is a maximum number of allowed participants at the Summer School, and therefore applicants will need to motivate their interest. The candidates will be selected based on their technical background and fit to the summer school and also considering diversity & inclusion aspects. eBISS 2027 adheres to the Diversity and Inclusion (D&I) initiative of the Database community aiming to guide researchers in the community to adopt a more inclusive mindset in general toward different individuals regardless of their age, gender, gender identity, race, cultural background, religion, physical and mental ability, sexual orientation, parental and marital status, etc.

Besides filling the application form, applicants will be asked to send a CV. The CV must contain in particular academic results (including marks) and English skills.
